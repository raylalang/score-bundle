## 2. Where the thesis stands
Three settings, each answered on held-out pieces. The attribution result
is the honest summary of what the graph does: two-beat smoothing. Two
gaps: which score structure beyond time matters, and what the
representation is for.

## 3. Three candidates, one proposal
The feedback view as first drawn is decoration: nothing in the model
changes. Its proper form is the across-performer model, which is real
work and is deferred. The motif study is bounded and can fail cleanly.

## 4. The deferred alternative
Shared piece mean plus per-performer deviation, both on the graph. The
band a feedback view needs is the second line: uncertainty about the
shared mean plus performer variation plus noise. ASAP has the repeated
performances. Deferred because it is a new model with its own
identifiability question.

## 5. The proposal, concept figure
Grey edges are what the graph has now. Blue edges are new: a note is
connected to the same position in every other occurrence of its motif.
The hidden note has its counterparts observed, which is the condition
the three spans never had.

## 6. The bridge
Occurrences of one type differ in length, so there is no clean
note-to-note map. Use relative position inside the occurrence instead:
a Gaussian in that coordinate, many to many, no alignment, nothing
learned. Then one learned weight, the Laplacian, the spectral kernel,
and the rest of the model unchanged. The evidence can switch the family
off.

## 7. The GP side
The additive kernel is a GMRF with precision I + sL. The conditional
mean of a hidden note is the degree-weighted mean of its neighbours,
which splits into a temporal part and a motif part; the variance is one
over one plus s times the degree. So motif edges change both the centre
and the width of the hidden note's band, which is why H1 is a
calibration hypothesis as well as a recovery one.

## 8. Methodology
Three arms, paired, annotations only. Masking targets motif notes while
another occurrence of the type stays observed. Data: 25 of the 32
annotated first movements are in ASAP. H1, H1b and the falsification
rule are written before the first run.

## 10. Stage one, first pilot (measured 4 November)
Development side only, no claim. Twenty-one Beethoven first movements,
the first 400 notes of one performance each, score features without the
music model, four arms on identical masks, two seeds, 336 fits, the
hypotheses in the script before the run. The decisive row is the second
one in panel A: whole occurrences hidden, the motif arm against the
time-only graph, minus 0.117 nats on timing with the interval just
clear of zero; the pitch term on the same notes does almost nothing.
Articulation accuracy improves five percent. Velocity calibration gets
worse, which says one weight shared by three channels is the wrong
design: a per-channel weight is the next step, not a retreat. Under
random masking nothing is visible, as expected: the time window already
interpolates inside an observed occurrence. Panel B: the evidence
switches the family off on eight pieces and asks for it on six, the
same per-piece behaviour the feature kernels show. What this is not: a
registered result, a thesis claim, or a test with the headline model's
embeddings.

## 11. Stage one, second pilot (measured 17 November)
Still development, still no claim. The question the first pilot left:
is the velocity penalty the shared weight? The per-channel form lets
the family enter as its own coregionalization block through the
feature-kernel path, one scale per channel learned by the evidence, no
change to the model code. Four seeds now; seeds 0 and 1 reproduce the
first pilot to the last digit, so the machinery is deterministic. The
answer in two halves. Velocity: the penalty is gone and becomes a gain,
coverage back at nominal, so that half of H2 holds. Timing: the
per-channel form gives nothing on the log score, and the rule written
before the run says the family's timing value came from the shared
coupling. Say the harder sentence plainly: on four seeds the shared
weight's own timing gain is minus 0.071 with an interval that covers
zero; the first pilot's star was two seeds and three pieces against
one. What survives everything is articulation accuracy, minus 0.035
shared and minus 0.019 per channel, both intervals clear of zero, and
panel B says it directly: the evidence keeps the family on the
articulation channel for ten pieces and switches it off on timing for
nineteen. Consequence: the pre-specified test on held-out performances
takes articulation RMSE as its primary, with power 0.95 at eighteen
pieces at this effect and 0.51 at two thirds; the shared weight is the
named arm because its articulation effect is larger and more even, and
the per-channel form runs beside it and is reported. The timing log
score is the secondary, power 0.33, reported either way.

## 12. Stage two: the ISMIR model (measured 17 November)
Walk the model top to bottom in one breath: typed graph and R-GCN
states, the two heads, label propagation with restart in training,
thresholded gated affinity and connected components at test time, GMM
grouping into types. Then the measurement, same day as the second pilot:
each piece's edges come from the fold that held it out, the masks and
the scored notes stay on the annotated occurrences, so the edge source
is the only change. The predicted membership matches the annotation at
a median F1 of 0.78, with two pieces where the model finds several
times too many motif notes. Timing log score minus 0.034, interval
reaching zero, 0.48 of the annotated effect on the same notes;
articulation RMSE minus 0.0135, four tenths of the annotated one. The
two edge sources act on related pieces, correlation 0.84 per piece on
articulation, 12 of 21 below zero for both. The rule written before the
run named the per-channel block: at least half the annotated timing
effect with an interval clear of zero. With predicted edges that block
moves timing the wrong way (+0.036 [−0.010, +0.095]), so the rule is not
supported (corrected 19 November; the earlier note scored the shared
form). The shared-form numbers above were recorded without a prediction.
The sentence to say: on a score without annotations, in the shared form,
the model's occurrences give about half the timing effect and four tenths
of the articulation effect, with intervals that include zero. Stage three, scores outside the annotated
corpus, is future work.
