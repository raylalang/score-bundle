## 2. Where the thesis stands
Three settings, each answered on held-out pieces. The attribution result
is the honest summary of what the graph does: two-beat smoothing. Two
gaps: which score structure beyond time matters, and what the
representation is for.

## 3. Three candidates, one proposal
The feedback view as first drawn is decoration: nothing in the model
changes. Its proper form is the across-performer model, which is real
work and is deferred. The motif study is bounded and can fail cleanly.

## 4. The deferred alternative
Shared piece mean plus per-performer deviation, both on the graph. The
band a feedback view needs is the second line: uncertainty about the
shared mean plus performer variation plus noise. ASAP has the repeated
performances. Deferred because it is a new model with its own
identifiability question.

## 5. The proposal, concept figure
Grey edges are what the graph has now. Blue edges are new: a note is
connected to the same position in every other occurrence of its motif.
The hidden note has its counterparts observed, which is the condition
the three spans never had.

## 6. The bridge
Occurrences of one type differ in length, so there is no clean
note-to-note map. Use relative position inside the occurrence instead:
a Gaussian in that coordinate, many to many, no alignment, nothing
learned. Then one learned weight, the Laplacian, the spectral kernel,
and the rest of the model unchanged. The evidence can switch the family
off.

## 7. The GP side
The additive kernel is a GMRF with precision I + sL. The conditional
mean of a hidden note is the degree-weighted mean of its neighbours,
which splits into a temporal part and a motif part; the variance is one
over one plus s times the degree. So motif edges change both the centre
and the width of the hidden note's band, which is why H1 is a
calibration hypothesis as well as a recovery one.

## 8. Methodology
Three arms, paired, annotations only. Masking targets motif notes while
another occurrence of the type stays observed. Data: 25 of the 32
annotated first movements are in ASAP. H1, H1b and the falsification
rule are written before the first run.

## 10. Stage two, later: the ISMIR model
Only if stage one gives an answer worth extending. Walk the model top to
bottom: typed graph and R-GCN states, the two heads, label propagation
with restart in training, thresholded gated affinity and connected
components at test time, GMM grouping into types. Its occurrences, spans
and types would replace the annotations on any score.

## 11. Plan and recommendation
Five working days, then one pre-specified evaluation if the development
result warrants it. Either outcome is a finding. Recommend the motif
study; the across-performer model goes to Future Work. The writing freeze
is reopened for this one study.
