# What belongs in the finished thesis

The largest cut is the repeated account of how each result earned its place in the project. The reader encounters the selection of an endpoint, the decision rule, the verdict and the adoption decision several times for a single comparison. Stage labels and status reports carry that history into chapter openings and captions. Together these two habits occupy roughly four to five pages. The largest individual opportunities are the introductory status table, the repeated URMP protocol, the preliminary waveform masking story and the serial account of the motif studies. Across the six categories I estimate **8–11 pages** can come out of the 75-page draft while preserving its held-out evidence.

Locations refer to [the audited thesis source](thesis/draft.tex) at `9cbfd77`. Each line range identifies the passage to edit. Longer quotations give its opening and closing words with an ellipsis between them. The action applies to that span. A caption or heading can keep its label when its wording changes.
