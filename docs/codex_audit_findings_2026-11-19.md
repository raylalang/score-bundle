# Audit of the waveform and motif results, 19 November 2026

The two held-out primary results agree with their decision rules. On 48 phrases from 20 previously used tracks, the graph lowers mean absolute intonation error by 0.82 cents against the temporal prior. The graph shows no detectable improvement over the independent prior with the same tuning offset. On 58 further performances of 18 development movements, annotated motif edges lower articulation RMSE on hidden returns by 0.0286. The secondary timing log-score prediction misses. The most consequential corrections concern the arm named in the predicted-occurrence hypothesis, the development RMSE values, and what information each evaluation uses.

The audit covers checkout `a58644c`, both registration documents at their named tags, the archived reports and saved predictions, and the local 89-page thesis and 45-page deck. Line numbers refer to this checkout unless a commit or tag is given. Proposed wording is for the manuscript, slides or notes. Where a narrative record and its generated table differ, both are identified.

24. **A scalar prior identity is used as a guarantee about the fitted predictor.** `docs/thesis/draft.tex:3207` says “the predictive band tightens by the extra degree” and line 3209 says “every motif arm coincides with the time-only graph”.     **Suggested wording** “At fixed scale and known neighbouring values, an increase in degree reduces the scalar latent conditional variance. 1. **What generalisation does the motif result test?** The thesis answers, “the evaluation is across performers of the same movements and not across repertoire” (`docs/thesis/draft.tex:3466`).
